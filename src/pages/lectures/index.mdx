# Lectures

Twelve lectures connect mechanisms, public evidence, and the decisions you
must make at 32M scale --- from what a language model learns, through
pre-training, post-training, and specialisation, to packaging a bounded
claim. Each lecture page names the teaching team member presenting it and
links to the weekly session and project it feeds.
